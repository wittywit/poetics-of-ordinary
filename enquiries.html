Enquiry No. 001 — On Thresholds

On the ritual of crossing a threshold — and what is left behind each time

A door is a machine for forgetting. The moment you cross it, the brain resets its context — what you were thinking, what you intended, where you were going. This is not failure. This is the threshold doing its work. We spent seven days paying deliberate attention to the moment of crossing.

Pranshu Chaudhary · March 2026 → Read Enquiry No. 006 — On Script

On handwriting and what is familiar in a stranger's script

When you read a handwritten note from someone you know well, you are reading two things simultaneously: the words, and the person. The loops, the pressure, the way certain letters lean — these are a second text, illegible to anyone who does not love the hand that made it. We asked: what makes handwriting feel like a person?
